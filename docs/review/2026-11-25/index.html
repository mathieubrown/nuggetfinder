Stamps, status and confidence

Three concepts need different language: chosen take stamp, crowd observation confidence, and verified store identity. Sauce Heretic / Certified Tender / Nope are playful opinions. UNCONFIRMED / IN STOCK / SOLD OUT / MIXED / STALE describe report agreement and freshness. VERIFIED STORE identifies an account/source. Cobalt SPONSORED identifies payment. None should confer the meaning of another.

The new local browser evidence shows one report as UNCONFIRMED and two matching people as IN STOCK. That is a pass. The independent audit also found that cached stock counts are not aged on the price page, so a once-valid IN STOCK label can remain displayed too long. This is a release correction, not a cosmetic suggestion. “Last seen” is an observation, never a guarantee that the next shopper will find it.

Before public participation: clarify opinion/verification and source freshness. Later polish: richer VS splash, warmer empty states, stronger cards and a grouped Discover destination.

Nugget Court decision brief

Proposal for review, not a rule change. Reviewed November 25, 2026 against d0174bb. Court is a playful community classification, not a food-safety, ingredient, inventory, business-verification or legal determination.

Recommendation

Keep seven days, 20 eligible accounts and 60% for an invited pilot, subject to your explicit decision. Do not lower the quorum simply to produce more winners. Treat low turnout and a divided jury as legitimate endings. Before public voting, define what happens when fraud or account deletion changes a closed result.

These numbers are product choices, not statistically validated measures of all shoppers' views. Twenty accounts are not proof of twenty independent humans. A 60% share in a self-selected poll is not scientific certainty.

What the code actually does

Start Seven days from first moderator approval, not nomination Keep this. Moderation delay should not consume voting time.

Closed-result integrity needs a decision

The independent local probe created a closed 12/5/3 result: NUGGET with 20 votes. Banning one Nugget voter changed it to NOT ENOUGH VOTES with 19 votes. No new vote was cast. Deletion can have the same effect through cascading vote removal.

Recommended future design: keep a versioned closing result, with a visible “Ruling under review” state if vote eligibility is challenged. A moderator can invalidate fraudulent votes and publish an explained new version; they cannot choose the winning side. Historical share cards should include a result date/version and should not silently contradict the current page. This is a proposed change requiring your decision, not implemented in this review.

Abuse resistance

Already present: sign-in, age-confirmed profile, bans/temporary locks, one row per account/case, server-side deadline checks, serialized account writes, cooldown, moderated nominations, blocked-term checks, report/block controls and duplicate-name prevention. Case authors may vote too; there is no author exclusion.

Missing or unproven: protection against one person creating many accounts, coordinated creator brigades, signup/email abuse controls, vote-action accounting, account-age rules, historical result versioning and a documented moderator response process. Blocking affects who can see a nomination, so two viewers can have different visibility; it does not make blocked accounts' votes disappear from aggregate counts merely because one viewer blocked them.

Recommendation: no purchase, sentiment or points reward for voting. No device fingerprint or location history. For an invited pilot, monitor suspicious activity manually and use reversible, explained moderation. If we add account-age eligibility or automated detection later, document false positives and appeals first. Email verification alone is not proof of a unique person.

Voice and information boundaries

Keep “Is it a nugget?”, “Court divided” and “Absolutely not.” Add a calm line on results and shares: “Community opinion · [count] votes · closed [date].” Never use VERIFIED or CONFIRMED to mean the food has objectively become a nugget. A winning katsu nomination does not verify allergens, recipe, nutrition, stock, price or a suggested location. All those need their own sources and timestamps.

Decisions needed from Mat

  1. Keep the proposed seven-day / 20-account / 60% pilot rules? Recommendation: yes, with no automatic extension or lower-quorum exception.
  2. Close low-turnout and tied/divided cases without a winner? Recommendation: yes; let the dialogue remain fun without manufacturing certainty.
  3. Use versioned results and a reviewed retrial process? Recommendation: yes. Decide who may request a retrial and what new reason is required; do not enable it silently.
  4. Does the daily limit cover 60 different cases or 60 vote changes? Recommendation: explicitly separate a case cap from an action cap, then test whichever rule you approve.
  5. Keep creator and author participation permitted? Recommendation: yes for opinion polls, with disclosed challenge links and no paid vote weighting.

Permissions, row security and account privacy

The local fixture intentionally grants broad default table privileges before applying all migrations. Final permission-matrix.json shows row security on all 34 public tables, no direct writes for anon/authenticated, whole-table SELECT only on products/places/place_products, and restricted column access on profiles. The tests reject banned/private profile fields, TRUNCATE, direct private-table reads/writes, self-promotion and forged store verification.

Private lists, trail progress, ratings, price submissions and receipts export only the caller's rows in the tested entry points. Public profiles intentionally expose handles/IDs and approved takes. Public prices mask handles but are not guaranteed anonymous: exact prices/timestamps and external posts can be correlated. No passive coordinate history is introduced by this stack; explicit user report/stamp records do link an account to a place/time and must be described accurately in privacy/retention policy.

Privileged functions check server identity, moderation roles or visibility and use fixed search paths. Older helpers using public rely on public-schema CREATE being revoked. Local tests show the final revocations, not the real project's role memberships/default grants. Schema CREATE, inherited roles, per-column privileges, sequence grants and every callable security-definer function must be compared against the actual production schema before release. The existing printed verifier covers much of this but is not an automated deployment gate.

No loaded production blocked-term list was inspected. Baseline local tests do not certify the quality or coverage of the private profanity/slur list. The list must remain out of git; a reviewed operational count/provenance check is still needed.

Retry and rollback assessment

Atomic receipt functions passed concurrent same-ID replay, changed-payload rejection, private exports and a browser lost-response test after commit. Server row locks serialize rate checks. The frontend also blocks duplicate submit events and preserves edits during a slow request. Older write functions intentionally remain callable, so a client can still create separate legitimate submissions with new IDs up to the normal limits.

Rollback should restore the previous frontend while retaining added schema/data. Old APIs exercised successfully against the candidate, but new hash routes will not work on an old frontend, and old hash routes currently fail on the new one (A07). These are distinct compatibility directions. Dropping new tables or undoing schema after users submit content is not a safe generic rollback. Restoring a backup can overwrite newer submissions and needs an explicit incident decision, recovery point and reconciliation plan.

The local synthetic restore passed. A production backup archive, offsite/privacy handling, managed Auth/storage recovery, role restoration, restore duration, recovery-point/recovery-time goals and actual production-data restoration remain unproven. No “rollback ready” signoff is warranted yet.
