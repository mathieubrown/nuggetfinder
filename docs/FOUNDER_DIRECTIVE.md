# Governing development directive

Revised by Mat on 2026-11-25. This supersedes older blanket age exclusion and deferred photography instructions. See [the implementation and release plan](participation-photo/PLAN.md) for the current build boundary and unresolved gates.

NuggetFinder is for children, teens and adults. Everyone should have the same core game: discovery, nugget and sauce ratings, Showdowns, Court, challenges, individual/family/private group competitions, stamps, trails, rankings, stock/price/discovery observations and appropriate food photography, takes, reactions and rebuttals. Votes have equal weight, with the same integrity controls. Age is not a ranking or vote multiplier.

Under-13 participation requires individually attributed pseudonymous parent-managed profiles, applicable verified parental consent, parent review, revocation and deletion. Teens need privacy defaults and protected interactions. Use server-enforced capabilities, not a single adult/minor switch. Never expose children's ages, names, schools, guardian relationships, location histories or identifying information publicly. No unsolicited adult-to-minor contact, public child discovery, contact uploads or direct messages. Explore private groups and expiring challenge codes without a public directory. Use illustrated avatars.

Food and product photography is Day Zero scope, integrated into reviews, ratings, cross-sections, stock/freezer observations, packaging, prices/promotions, menus, redacted receipts, Showdowns, Court exhibits, trails/challenges, takes and rebuttals. Shared evidence identity links these contexts. Observation and submission time are separate. Photos are evidence, not automatic stock confirmation, price verification, authenticity or fact. Preserve source attribution, moderation, corrections, removal, erasure and minimal provenance.

Initially publish food/products only for every age. No identifiable people, selfies, personal profile photos or personal videos. Server-side validation/re-encoding and metadata removal are mandatory, as are private staging, crop/opaque redaction, prepublication human moderation, reporting, deletion, upload limits and defined cleanup. Automated screening assists; it cannot certify safety or ban people. No face-based age estimation. Video remains deferred.

Keep the sparkle, stamps, irreverence and competition. Roast the nugget, not the person. Age-appropriate language safeguards and structured funny responses should preserve personality without exposing children to adult language or harassment.

All existing security corrections remain. Court stays seven days, 20 voters, 60%, no forced winners, append-only auditable corrections. Keep stock confidence, price recency, source verification and catalog integrity distinct. Account deletion is mandatory. Initial participation is invitation-only. Launch geography is SF, Daly City and South San Francisco/Peninsula. Sponsorships are labeled and never affect organic ranking. No user-level data sales/sharing, paid services or LLM calls without approval.

Enable each age group's capabilities only after its applicable consent, privacy and safety requirements are implemented and tested. Closed gates are implementation dependencies, not a decision to exclude children or defer photos. Do not ship an adult-only release as fulfillment of this directive.

Development and isolated testing are authorized. All production SQL, migrations, merges, deployments, settings changes and live participation remain held until Mat explicitly approves them. Never use production credentials to run local tests.
