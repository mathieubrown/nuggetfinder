# Stripe billing for brand customers

Accessed November 14, 2026. Confidence: high for published US rates, medium for the eventual payment mix.

Invoicing Starter charges 0.4% per paid invoice. Plus charges 0.5% and adds quotes. Billing pay as you go charges 0.7% of recurring Billing volume and excludes one-off invoices. Payment processing is a separate cost. At $1,000 paid, invoice fees are $4 or $5 and Billing is $7 before processing. With standard US domestic card pricing of 2.9% + $0.30, total modeled fees are $33.30, $34.30 and $36.30. ACH rates and caps differ. [Stripe Invoicing pricing](https://stripe.com/invoicing/pricing) · [Stripe Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing) · [Stripe payments pricing](https://stripe.com/pricing)

Use an invoice for a one-time campaign with explicit deliverables. Consider subscriptions once brand customers have a recurring plan with clear cancellation terms. Keep billing access server-side and verify webhooks before granting entitlements.
