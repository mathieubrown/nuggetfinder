# California privacy and aggregate data

Accessed November 14, 2026. Confidence: high for agency thresholds and statutory definitions, medium for the effect of a proposed dashboard.

The CCPA business definition includes alternative tests for a qualifying for-profit business doing business in California. The revenue threshold, adjusted effective January 2025, is over $26,625,000 in the preceding calendar year. Other tests include annually buying, selling or sharing personal information of at least 100,000 consumers or households, or deriving at least 50% of annual revenue from selling or sharing consumers' personal information. Consult counsel on related entities and applicable exceptions. [CalPrivacy adjustment](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/cpi_adjustment.html) · [CCPA statute](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute.pdf)

The statute treats aggregate consumer information as group-level information without identities reasonably linkable to a consumer or household. Deidentified data also requires reasonable measures, a public commitment against reidentification and contractual restrictions for recipients. A minimum of 10 is a product rule. Review repeated queries, sparse neighborhoods and joinability separately. [CCPA statute](https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute.pdf)

If a dashboard launches, describe source data, aggregate outputs, recipients and uses accurately. If applicable, disclosures for personal information include categories collected and disclosed, purposes, retention and rights, plus sale/share disclosures and opt-out methods where relevant. Review the updated regulations before publishing. [California AG FAQ](https://www.oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa) · [CalPrivacy updates](https://www.cppa.ca.gov/regulations/ccpa_updates)
