# FTC endorsements and review rules

Accessed November 14, 2026. Confidence: high for the text, medium for applying it to each placement.

The Endorsement Guides explain clear, conspicuous disclosure of a material connection when the audience would not expect it. Creators should disclose payment, free goods or other material ties with the endorsement itself. A platform running a sponsored campaign should provide a clear disclosure on each placement, tell creators what to disclose, and monitor compliance. For video, review how the claim and disclosure appear and sound together. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews) · [16 CFR Part 255](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255)

The Reviews and Testimonials Rule addresses fake or false reviews, incentives tied to sentiment, certain insider reviews, company-controlled sites purporting to be independent, suppression, and fake indicators of social influence. Section 465.4 refers to reviews “expressing a particular sentiment, whether positive or negative.” For Nuggetfinder, paid placements should visibly say Sponsored and Paid by the named brand. Rewards for confirmed actions should apply equally to opposing reports. [16 CFR Part 465](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-465)
