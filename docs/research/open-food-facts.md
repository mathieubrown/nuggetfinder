# Open Food Facts seed options

Accessed November 14, 2026. Confidence: high for published API guidance, medium for applying the database license to our schema.

The current developer guide lists 15 product reads and 10 searches per IP per minute. Identify the application with a custom User-Agent, cache repeat lookups and keep typing-driven searches local. Use published bulk exports for more than a few hundred products. The main terms page returned a crawler denial in this review, so check it directly before importing. [API guide](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/) · [Terms of use](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use)

The database is ODbL, individual contents are covered by the Database Contents License, and product photos have separate attribution and share-alike terms. Credit Open Food Facts and contributors with links back to the data source and relevant license. Keep source and barcode provenance, and keep any share-alike dataset independently exportable. Do not present imported allergen fields as confirmed. [Official license guide](https://openfoodfacts.github.io/documentation/docs/Product-Opener/api/tutorials/license-be-on-the-legal-side/)

Counsel questions: Does joining an imported product subset to our stock data build an adapted database? What source links and photo credits must appear in the product UI, exports and share cards?
