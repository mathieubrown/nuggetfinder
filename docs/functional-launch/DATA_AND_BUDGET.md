# Pilot data and budget

Prices checked November 26, 2026 in USD. This is a planning estimate for an invitation-only web pilot. The monthly subtotal assumes a single production database, one worker host sized for a small pilot, and light email usage.

| Item | Starting cost | Decision |
|---|---:|---|
| Database, Auth and private storage | $25/month | Supabase Pro is the production budget baseline. It includes one Micro instance under the stated compute credit and daily database backups with seven-day retention. Confirm the current project tier and storage/transfer against actual measured usage. An additional continuously running Micro project can add $10/month. [Supabase pricing](https://supabase.com/pricing) |
| API and isolated photo worker | $12–18/month | Budget example pricing for a 2 GiB Linux VM with one or two vCPUs. Load-test concurrent photos and worker timeouts before selecting a size. Any dedicated worker, monitoring or off-host backup storage increases cost. [DigitalOcean VM pricing](https://www.digitalocean.com/pricing/droplets) |
| Transactional email | $0 initially or $20/month | A production SMTP sender and verified sending domain are needed for sign-in and recovery. Resend's free tier lists 3,000 emails monthly and 100 daily. Pro starts at $20 for 50,000 emails. Configure domains only after approval. [Supabase custom SMTP](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp), [Resend pricing](https://resend.com/pricing) |
| Domain | Quote after checking ownership | Reuse a domain you already own if suitable. Quote registration and renewal for the selected domain and separately configure the sending subdomain. |
| Consent, legal and human review | Quote and staff time | Choose the independently verified consent method with counsel. Provider pricing and vendor agreement terms depend on the method and volume. Staff time includes image, shop, claim, safety and appeal review. |
| Maps and catalog | $0 paid API spend in the pilot plan | The dated OSM extract serves candidate searches today. Before increasing usage, select a reviewed tile provider or host tiles and measure traffic. Paid retailer inventory feeds are an optional later integration. [OSM tile policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) |
| Apple Developer Program | $99/year when pursuing App Store or TestFlight distribution | Responsive web runs through a browser. Native iPhone distribution is a separate decision. [Apple Developer enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/) |

Baseline for database, VM and free SMTP is **$37–43/month**, or **$57–63/month** with the paid SMTP tier. Other line items above, taxes and potential usage charges are separate. Keep a small pilot until hosting and moderation load are measured. Twenty photos a day averaging 0.5 MB across stored variants would use about 300 MB per 30 days before erasure. At ten 0.5 MB views per upload, that would be approximately 3 GB of photo transfer per month. Those are scenarios to measure, with actual storage and backup lifetimes governed by the approved retention plan. At 45 seconds per photo, twenty daily photos add 15 minutes of review per day ahead of other queues.

## What the pilot data actually covers

The fresh catalog has 25 products, 83 approved places, and 189 product/place links. The 96 places-to-check links are shopping leads. Every seeded product has at least one place link. Four places have no address label. The fresh database has zero stock, price or allergen evidence rows. The November 7 snapshot adds 4,609 searchable OSM candidates whose business facts need review before approval. The audit is in [catalog-audit.json](catalog-audit.json).

The product and listing submission paths now address missing items and missing product/store pairs without waiting for an import. At pilot start, prioritize the stores people can actually visit in SF, South City and Daly City. Review the four missing addresses first. Review store brand links for the correct retailer, and verify names, package sizes and official URLs against the source notes for packaged products. Have reviewers mark uncertain locations or menus as awaiting evidence.

Refresh search candidates with `scripts/extract-place-search.py` using a newer reviewed regional PBF extract. Check its date, hash and bounds, compare added/changed/removed OSM IDs, and preserve approved IDs and historic observations. Refreshing the search index does not overwrite the approved catalog. Use moderator corrections for approved addresses and closures. Restock, sellout and price evidence should come from dated independent observations or from separately labeled verified businesses. Real-time inventory accuracy varies by store and requires a participating source.
