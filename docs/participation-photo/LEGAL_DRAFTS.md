# Participation and photo legal drafts

DRAFT, NEEDS LAWYER REVIEW. Not an operative notice or a statement of compliance. No collection, consent or public photo service is enabled by this text. Counsel must approve actual practices, contact details and retention before publication.

## Draft terms addition

NuggetFinder is a food discovery game and community. Community votes and Court rulings express opinions. Stock and prices are dated observations, not guarantees. Photos add context and do not independently verify availability, authenticity, pricing or allergens. Upload only your own appropriate food/product images and content you have authority to submit. Do not include identifiable people, private documents, payment information or personal details. Redact receipts before submission. Illustrated avatars replace personal profile photographs. Video publication is unavailable.

Roast the nugget, not the person. Harassment, sexual content, threats, personal insults, deceptive reports and sharing private information are prohibited. Moderators may reject or remove photos, restrict participation and review appeals under published rules. Businesses may respond but cannot remove user opinions. Sponsored material is labeled and never changes organic votes or rankings.

Age-appropriate capabilities depend on implemented consent and safety requirements. Children use parent-managed pseudonymous identities with applicable verified parental consent. Private groups do not create permission for unsolicited contact. Terms must address guardian authority, content licensing limited to operating the service, removal/withdrawal, dispute handling and age-appropriate assent. Counsel must write enforceable language and determine who can agree to what.

## Draft parent notice structure

Before applicable child collection: identify operator and contact; explain requested data, purposes and services; distinguish integral processing from optional disclosure; explain the specific verification method; explain who can see contributions; provide review, pause, revocation, export and deletion routes; state approved retention and backup expiry. Do not imply a parent email, invitation, child's attestation or successful payment automatically verifies consent. No ID images or payment credentials in application tables or moderation queues. Verification receipts contain only the reviewed minimum.

Parents should control each child's capabilities independently. Granting photo submission must not silently grant public place/time attribution or social discoverability. Withdrawal must immediately stop new actions and public serving, then trigger reviewed erasure rules. Explain safety limits of photos, redaction and human review. A consent workflow must remain unavailable until its server verification and revocation tests pass.

## Draft privacy addition

Minimize identity data. Private age band and guardian relationship are used for permission checks, never public profiles or rankings. No public ages, real names, schools, personal location histories, contact uploads or direct messages. Child contributions must not produce a public visit timeline. Current external fonts, scripts, map tiles, authentication emails, hosting logs and persistent identifiers require a reviewed vendor inventory and child-safe configuration. Do not describe third parties as absent merely because analytics has not been added.

Photographs require server validation and metadata stripping, private processing, human review and purpose-bound display. Raw files are not published. The proposed retention schedule in PLAN.md is not yet operational; counsel and engineering must approve and implement exact periods and exceptions. Name actual providers and subprocessors after selection, not hypothetical AI providers. No photo training use or sale/sharing of individual information is planned. Receipt, account and child data requests need verified requester authority and an accessible process.

## Draft moderation and copyright contact addition

Provide a clearly working image report route for people, private information, non-food content and rights complaints. Stop public serving promptly on the relevant removal decision. Preserve only the minimum lawful safety/decision record, not a permanent archive of rejected images. Explain appeal and correction review. A copyright agent/contact and operational response process must be established before public uploads; existing placeholder DMCA contacts are a release blocker. [Copyright Office agent directory](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/), accessed 2026-11-25, describes agent registration; counsel must assess the applicable process and protections.

## Questions requiring counsel

- Is the intended product child-directed, mixed audience or another classification, and what does that require before identifiers, maps or authentication are used?
- Which age-assurance and parent-verification methods are appropriate for these purposes and disclosure choices? What independent consent/reconsent, age-transition and withdrawal records are necessary?
- Which California child/teen rules currently apply, including applicability thresholds, injunctions and effective dates? What restrictions on profiling, precise location, sale/sharing and vendor contracts follow for this product?
- Can public child food observations safely use pseudonyms and coarse time/place aggregates, and what constitutes identifying location history in this design?
- How should parent/teen review rights, sibling/shared devices, guardian disputes and privacy rights interact?
- What image rights/license, copyright contact/agent, retention, abuse evidence and backup deletion practices are required? What lawful exceptions need tightly limited access and documentation?
